Model notes

The surveillance model pools case reports from this facility's intake records against historical seasonal baselines. An area is flagged High when its 7-day case count exceeds the seasonal threshold by more than 30%, sustained across 3+ reporting days.

In production, this connects to multi-facility reporting and national HMIS feeds for wider catchment visibility.
